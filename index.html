01 · THE ETHOS

Not a kitten factory.
A long-term responsibility.

Angeliana's public position is unusually clear: breeding should be controlled, documented and centred on the animal's wellbeing. Kittens are not rushed out early, buyers are screened, and unsuitable homes can be turned away.

This site makes that philosophy the brand itself instead of presenting Kucingliar KL as another generic pet catalogue.

01

Health first

Vaccination, blood screening and health documentation form part of the placement standard Angeliana described publicly.

02

Slow breeding

A deliberately limited breeding cycle that puts recovery and welfare ahead of volume.

03

Home matching

Prospective owners are screened for household environment, existing pets and long-term suitability.

04

Education

Social content turns day-to-day care, feeding, hygiene and responsible ownership into practical learning.

05 · BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

The right kitten needs the right home.

This is intentionally not an add-to-cart experience. The enquiry flow asks enough to filter serious homes first and make the welfare standard part of the customer journey.

✓

Your cat will live primarily as a household companion, not permanently in a cage.

✓

You are comfortable with routine veterinary care, vaccination and quality nutrition.

✓

You can share your current pet setup, household and experience honestly.

✓

You are choosing for long-term fit, not just colour, trend or immediate availability.
