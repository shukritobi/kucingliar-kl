# Research notes — Angeliana Dennis / Kucingliar KL / MAFEAA

Research refreshed: 2026-10-03

## Identity
The Rich Choice Holistic booth in the supplied screenshot should not be treated as Angeliana's company. The stronger public trail points to Kucingliar KL as the creator/business identity and MAFEAA as the cattery naming prefix associated with Angeliana Dennis Timban and Muhamed Khairul Adam.

## Publicly supported facts used in this prototype
- Free Malaysia Today profiled Angeliana Dennis Timban as a licensed British Shorthair breeder and reported that she screens buyers.
- FMT reported that kittens are typically placed at around five to six months after three vaccine doses, blood tests and a health certificate.
- FMT reported one litter per female cat per year and that cage-only homes can be rejected.
- FMT reported more than 20 cats in her care.
- FIFe World Show 2025 records list MAFEAA IPAN and MAFEAA INAI as British Shorthair entries owned by Angeliana Binti Dennis Timban / Muhamed Khairul Adam.
- Feline Fanciers' Federation records list MAFEAA DAMAI UCHIHA LISUT in 2024 winner records.
- A public Maybank campaign record lists Kucingliar KL Enterprise.
- Creator analytics pages in September 2026 reported @kucingliar_kl at more than 600K TikTok followers. These are third-party estimates, not first-party platform analytics.
- Supplied screenshots show Angeliana using @siput_comel on Instagram and Threads.
